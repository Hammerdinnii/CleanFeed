Fixing my bike

So the rear derailleur on my old Trek finally gave up last week. I'd been ignoring the clicking for months, which, in hindsight, was dumb. Took it apart on the balcony, lost one tiny spring under the radiator and spent twenty minutes on my knees with a flashlight. Found it eventually.

Turns out the hanger was bent, probably from when I dropped it at the train station in March. A new hanger was eleven bucks. The shop guy said I could've bent it back but he wouldn't trust it, and honestly neither would I. Rode it to work today and it shifts fine now, except third gear still makes a weird noise when it's cold out. I'll deal with that later. Or never. We'll see.
